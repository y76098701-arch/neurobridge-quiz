    Sample Article: How The Human Brain Stores Knowledge

    Memory formation is an astonishing biological symphony. When we learn new facts or challenge our minds with interactive quizzes, neurons communicate through microscopic junctions called synapses. Repetitive retrieval strengthens these synaptic pathways through Long-Term Potentiation (LTP). This active recall rewires our brain, ensuring that acquired wisdom remains vivid and accessible for years to come.
